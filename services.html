Do I need an appointment? +

Consultations are by appointment so you receive focused, unhurried attention. Call +91-98260-78711 or +91-97544-78711, or send the enquiry form.

How long does a custom piece take? +

Timelines vary with design complexity, fabric sourcing and fitting rounds. Share your event date in the enquiry and we will advise honestly whether it is achievable.

Can I bring a reference or an existing outfit? +

Yes — references, heirloom pieces and fabric you love are welcome starting points. We adapt them into a silhouette that fits you.
