Welcome to Ella™

Crafted for women who define their own style.

Ella™ Designer Boutique is a fashion design studio in Indore, led by Fashion Designer Swaleha Khan. Every silhouette is designed around you — your occasion, your personality, your fit. From refined day wear to statement couture and bridal, we create elegant, personalised fashion with careful craftsmanship and sophisticated finishing.
