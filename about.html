About Ella

The art of
personal style.

Ella™ Designer Boutique is a designer studio in Indore associated with Fashion Designer Swaleha Khan — creating elegant, personalised fashion for women who value fit, detail and individuality.

The Boutique

A studio, not a store.

Ella is built around the design process — conversation, sketch, fabric, trial and finish. Rather than racks of generic sizes, you experience a considered one-on-one journey where each garment is shaped to your body, occasion and temperament. The result is quiet luxury: clean lines, thoughtful embellishment and a fit that feels made for you — because it is.

Fashion Designer

Swaleha Khan

Design led by listening.

Designer Boutique by Fashion Designer Swaleha Khan. Her work begins with understanding — how you live, celebrate and carry yourself — before a single line is sketched. We do not publish an invented biography here; we let the garments and the experience speak: personal attention, honest guidance on fabric and silhouette, and meticulous follow-through to the final fitting.

Design Philosophy

Individuality, elegance and detail — in every stitch.

01

Made for one

Each design starts from your measurements, occasion and preference — never mass-produced.

02

Elegance first

Restrained embellishment, graceful drape and refined colour — sophistication over excess.

03

Detail obsessed

Seams, linings, falls and finishes are resolved with couture-level care.

The Experience

Personalised, unhurried, precise.

01 Consultation

Share your occasion, references and comfort. We guide you on silhouette, fabric and tone at the Indore studio or by phone.

02 Design & Fabric

Sketch direction and curated fabric options selected for drape, season and celebration.

03 Craft & Fitting

Pattern, stitching and trial fittings to refine fit, fall and detail before final finishing.

04 Final Look

Pressed, finished and styled — ready for your moment, with fitting support.
